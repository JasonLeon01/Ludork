`applyFromMapData` reads the optional `fog` field as a string and `fogPower`, `fogOx`, `fogOy` and `fogDistort` as finite numbers representable by `float`. A missing or `nil` `fog` becomes an empty string, and a missing or `nil` numeric field becomes `0`. A blank `fog` or a `fogPower` at or below `0` disables fog. `fogPower` and `fogDistort` are floored and clamped to `0..100`; `fogOx` and `fogOy` are the scroll speeds. Provided values must have the declared type: numeric strings, scalar-to-string conversion, non-finite numbers and numbers outside the float range are rejected with the field path.
