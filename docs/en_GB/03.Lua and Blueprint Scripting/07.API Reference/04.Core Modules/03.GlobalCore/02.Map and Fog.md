`MapFogSettings` accepts a Lua table with `fog` (string) and `fogPower`, `fogOx`, `fogOy`, `fogDistort` (finite numbers in the float range). Missing or nil fields default to `""` and `0`, respectively; extra map fields are ignored. Invalid types or ranges raise an error. Blank `fog` or `fogPower <= 0` disables fog. `fogPower` and `fogDistort` are floored and clamped to `0..100`; `fogOx` and `fogOy` are scroll speeds. `applyFromMapData`, `applyWorldFromMapData` and `System.applyFogFromMapData` share this configuration.

## WorldStreamingState

`WorldRegionState` exposes `Unloaded`, `Reading`, `Prepared`, `Active` and `Dormant`; `WorldRegionDemand` exposes `None`, `Prepared` and `Active`. `WorldStreamingState` uses 1-based indices and owns demand/queues, Camera ordering (Active first, then squared centre distance, with region index as the stable tie-break), last-used/cache accounting, eviction and statistics. Its methods are `metadata = false`; Blueprint uses contextual world-map nodes.

Lua calls `updateCameraCenter`/`updateDemand`, consumes `takeReadBatch`/`takePublishItem`, reports read/publish completion or cancellation, marks activation/deactivation/eviction, and reads `getEvictionList`/`getStats`; files, builders and payloads remain Lua-owned.
