## Slider

Direct metadata bases: `{ "Engine", "ControlBase" }`, `{ "Engine", "FunctionalBase" }`

### Touch interaction

A Slider starts adjusting as soon as a primary touch begins inside its effective touch hit bounds. The beginning position sets the value immediately, so a tap selects and adjusts the Slider on its first contact. Beginning, tapping and dragging all derive the value from the pointer's X coordinate against the Slider's visual bounds. Y does not affect the value and, after capture, does not cancel the adjustment.

Once captured, the Slider keeps adjusting from X movement until release even when the pointer moves outside the touch hit bounds in either axis. Positions beyond the left or right edge clamp to the range minimum or maximum. Release ends the drag without transferring the gesture to another control. The inherited `setTouchHitBounds` API may enlarge the touch target without changing the rendered Slider or its mouse hit bounds.
