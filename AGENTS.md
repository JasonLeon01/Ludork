Shared policy lives in the business checkout's [AGENTS.md](https://github.com/JasonLeon01/Ludork/blob/main/AGENTS.md): read its Working agreement, Documentation workflow, Documentation authoring, and Execution and completion sections before working here, including documentation-only tasks.
